Limits of the calculation

The calculator models a simply supported steel W-beam under the entered downward dead and live loads and beam self-weight, using the assumptions shown in the calculator. Its checks are limited to the selected calculation scope. It does not design or verify connections, bearing, local web forces, lateral-bracing assemblies, supports, the complete scaffold system, or all required load combinations. It does not analyze axial forces or applied torsion.

Wind, snow, rain, and earthquake loads are not added or checked separately. If you enter such a load as dead or live load, the calculator treats it only as a downward static load. The loading, restraints, and other conditions must be checked for the actual project, including any destabilizing effect of load on an unbraced top flange.
